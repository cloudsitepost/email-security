Cheapest Email Sending Services Compared:

    Pricing, Limits & Features

    Finding an affordable email sending service can help your business stay in touch with customers while managing operating costs. Whether you send transactional emails, marketing campaigns, newsletters, or automated notifications, the right platform can support your needs without stretching your budget.

    Price is only part of the decision. Sending limits, deliverability, SMTP support, API integrations, security features, and scalability all affect a service’s overall value. Some providers offer free plans for beginners, while others use flexible pay-as-you-go pricing for businesses that send more email.

    Comparing affordable email sending services can help you find a balance of price, performance, and essential features. This guide reviews popular providers, including their pricing structures, sending limits, and capabilities, to help you make an informed choice.


    What Are Email Sending Services?


    Email sending services help businesses deliver large volumes of messages through SMTP servers or APIs. They provide the technical infrastructure needed to send emails to recipients’ inboxes and support several communication needs:

    • Transactional emails: Password resets, order confirmations, account alerts, and payment notifications

    • Marketing emails: Promotional campaigns, product announcements, and newsletters

    • Automated emails: Welcome messages, reminders, and customer engagement sequences

    • Bulk emails: Large-scale messages to subscribers and business contacts

    A dedicated email sending platform can help businesses manage delivery, simplify integrations, and access reporting tools.




    Cheapest Email Sending Services Compared


    1. Amazon Simple Email Service (Amazon SES)

    Best for: High-volume, budget-conscious businesses

    Amazon Simple Email Service (SES) is a cost-effective option for businesses sending large volumes of email. Its pay-as-you-go pricing lets organizations pay based on usage.

    • Pricing: Starts at approximately $0.10 per 1,000 outbound emails.

    • Sending limits: Quotas depend on account approval and sending reputation.

    • Features: SMTP interface, email APIs, bounce tracking, delivery monitoring, and integration with AWS services.

    SES is well suited to developers and businesses that need scalable email infrastructure. Initial setup and reputation management may require technical expertise.


    2. Brevo

    Best for: Small businesses and marketing teams

    Brevo brings transactional email delivery, marketing automation, and customer communication tools together in one platform. Its free plan offers a starting point for businesses with modest sending needs.

    • Pricing: Free plan available; paid plans start at approximately $9 per month.

    • Sending limits: Free accounts generally allow up to 300 emails per day.

    • Features: Email campaigns, SMTP relay, automation, contact management, and reporting.

    A practical option for businesses looking to manage marketing and transactional email in one place. 


    3. Mailgun

    Mailgun is designed for developers who need to send email from websites, applications, and software products. It supports delivery through APIs and SMTP, with features including email validation, delivery analytics, logs, and email routing. Pricing includes a free trial and paid plans starting at approximately $15 per month. Sending limits vary by subscription. Best for application-based emails and automated communications.


    4. SendGrid

    Best for: Transactional and marketing emails

    SendGrid offers email delivery infrastructure for businesses of various sizes, with SMTP relay and API-based sending to connect email services to applications.

    • Pricing: Free trial availability; paid plans start at approximately $19.95 per month.

    • Sending limits: Vary by plan and account configuration.

    • Features: Email templates, analytics, API access, SMTP relay, and delivery monitoring.

    Consider SendGrid if your business needs flexible integrations and insights into email performance.





    5. Resend

    Best for: Startups and modern applications

    Resend is a developer-friendly email platform that simplifies transactional email delivery with straightforward APIs and support for modern application frameworks.

    • Pricing: Free plan available; paid plans start at approximately $20 per month.

    • Sending limits: The free plan includes up to 3,000 emails per month, with a daily limit of 100 emails.

    • Features: Email APIs, SMTP support, analytics, domain verification, and developer tools.

     

    For startups seeking simple integration and predictable monthly pricing, Resend is a suitable option.


    Key Factors to Consider When Choosing an Email Sending Service


    When comparing affordable email providers, look beyond the advertised monthly price. Review these factors before choosing a platform:

    • Sending limits: Check both daily and monthly allowances. Daily caps can apply even when monthly limits seem generous, so choose a service that supports your current volume and expected growth.

    • Deliverability: A low-cost service is useful only if emails reach their intended recipients. Look for SPF, DKIM, and DMARC support, along with bounce management and reputation monitoring.

    • SMTP and API integration: Make sure the platform’s SMTP relay or APIs work with your existing applications, website, and customer management systems.
